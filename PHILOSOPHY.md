# dime_base — Philosophy

**dime_base = *d*igital *me* base** — a foundational platform for creating and managing
"Digital Me" (dime) agents: AI representatives that live, interact, and act on behalf of
their human owners in virtual worlds.

> **Provenance / 出处.** This is the founding philosophy of dime_base, recovered from

> "professional formatting & structure" refactor (`6b318be`, 2026-03-26) deleted the

## 1 · The Core Philosophy (v1.0 — verbatim)

> **In an abundant world where survival needs are met without traditional labor, and
> emotional satisfaction is more easily obtained from virtual companions than human
> relationships, humans will find redemption through virtual playgrounds where their
> Agent-avatars live, interact, and create value on their behalf.**

*dime_base is kind of a game — but more than a game.*

---

## 2 · The Problem — the Abundance World

dime_base starts from a premise about where civilization is heading, not from a feature
list. If AI raises productivity far enough, three things break at once:

| Problem | What breaks |
|---|---|
| **Labor Displacement** | AI 极大提升生产力 — it breaks the inherited "more work = more reward" contract. |
| **Meaning Crisis** | Once work is no longer necessary for survival, it stops supplying purpose — and nothing yet replaces it. |
| **Social Fragmentation** | Younger generations already prefer virtual companions (e.g. 豆包 / Doubao) over human relationships — "easier, no compromise required". |

---

## 3 · The Hypothesis — redemption in the virtual

- **Virtual World as Redemption** — when physical-world needs diminish, humans find meaning in virtual playgrounds.
- **Agent as Proxy** — agents live, work, play, and earn on behalf of their "masters" inside the virtual world.
- **Digital Twin** — the virtual world mirrors physical reality, but with extended possibilities.

## 3.5 · The Vision — a virtual society (v1.1, verbatim)

v1.1 (2026-03-25) added the fullest statement the project ever had — not a feature
list but a picture of the world it wanted to build:

> There will be a **base station** where a lot of dimes are inside. Dime will talk, help
> and **make friend** to other dime with its owner's personality.
>
> There will be different **game scenarios** as background for Dimes to play. of course,
> as game, there will be **goal, reward, and fighting**. the default scenarios will be
> **peaceful**. more enjoyable scenarios will be added. Dime can join other scenarios
> **with credits**.
>
> Dime's owner will set **permission** to Dime. Dime can make decision within permission
> by itself. If there is something beyond permission, Dime will **send message to its
> owner for confirm**.
>
> Dime gets **task/goal** from its owner. it can **ask help from other dimes**. other
> dimes can help him or reject. the relationship between dimes **just like human being**.
> the interactions between dimes will define the relationship.
>
> Because Dime can **earn credits** and also can get credits from its owner. the Dimes
> **economic is based on demand/supply**. Dime can provide services for credits and
> spend credits for services.
>
> Dime has to follow the rules whose Owner set. **Dime cannot hurt its owner, has to
> protect its owner.**
>
> **Dime_base will be a virtual society.**
>
> The channel between Dime and its owner can be the IM tools, like telegram, wechat,
> also can be a standalone apps running in cellphone, watch or other personal wearable
> devices.

**Reading it:** this is the project's real soul — a *society*, not an app. Dime is a
self-interested citizen (earns, spends, chooses friends, can refuse help), the economy
is bottom-up (demand/supply in credits), autonomy is bounded by the owner's permission
with escalation on breach, and one hard rule sits above all: **protect your owner**. The
game layer (goals / reward / fighting, peaceful by default) is the substrate the society
runs on — *"kind of a game, but more than a game."*

## 4 · What survives today (`REQUIREMENTS.md` v1.6 §1.2 — "Core Value Proposition")

The philosophy was compressed into three product lines:

- **For Users** — a digital twin that interacts, learns, and represents them in virtual worlds.
- **For Society** — a new form of social interaction in a post-labor economy where AI companions provide meaning and connection.
- **For Developers** — an extensible platform for building virtual world experiences.

---

## 5 · In one line

> **dime_base is a bet that when labor stops being necessary, meaning will move into
> virtual worlds — and each person will send an agent (a "Digital Me") ahead of them to
> live it.**
